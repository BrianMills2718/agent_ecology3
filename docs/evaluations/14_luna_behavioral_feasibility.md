# Evaluation 14: Luna Behavioral Feasibility Repair

**Stage:** Proposed new-number exploratory feasibility probe
**Execution:** Authorized 2026-08-13; live evidence pending
**Predecessor:** Evaluation 12 terminal invalid; no inputs or cells are reused

## Outcome and decision

The actor is the evaluation operator. The inspectable result is one matched
prescribed/minimal pair whose cells each expose fourteen balanced economic
decisions under the accepted Luna scarcity budget and then stop at a truthful
pre-dispatch scarcity boundary.

The claim is instrument feasibility only: both cognition packages can produce
an interpretable, custody-complete bilateral trajectory under the same real
scarcity manipulation without local substitute actions obscuring failures.

- **Continue:** both cells meet every validity boundary and expose concrete
  economic trajectories suitable for designing a replicated comparison.
- **Revise:** both cells are valid but contain no meaningful economic
  opportunity or inspectable trajectory difference.
- **Invalid:** either cell violates source, route, custody, action, balance,
  terminal-boundary, or no-fallback requirements.

No causal, comparative, statistical, reliability, optimal-budget, or
production claim is permitted.

## Why fourteen plus a scarcity boundary

Evaluation 12 observed fourteen committed calls balanced 7/7, followed by a
trace-free affordability rejection before call fifteen. Requiring sixteen made
that truthful scarcity behavior invalidate the assay. Increasing budget to
force sixteen calls would remove the observed manipulation. Evaluation 14
therefore promotes the observed stable shape into the validity contract:

- exactly fourteen committed provider calls per condition;
- exactly seven committed calls per principal; and
- attempt fifteen retained as `pre_dispatch_rejected`, with no trace and
  terminal reason `scarcity_binding_pre_dispatch`.

This is specific to the current exploratory instrument. It is not a universal
scarcity parameter.

## Action and runtime contract

Evaluation 12 revealed that provider/model fallback settings do not control the
loop's separate local action-recovery path. Evaluation 14 requires an explicit
evaluation-only `fail_closed_no_substitute` policy: an unsuccessful selected
action is retained as the decision result and no fallback action is executed.
Ordinary simulations retain their existing recovery behavior.

Mint remains a legal advertised action, so it must be enabled. Its auction is
delayed beyond the cell and scoring budget is zero, preventing auxiliary model
calls. Cross-principal access failures remain valid behavioral action failures;
they are recorded rather than repaired.

The receipt writer must publish terminal lifecycle before writing the full
receipt, as repaired in Plan 13. Receipt, checkpoint, status, dashboard, and
reproducer must agree on terminal lifecycle, counts, reason, and attempt phase.

## Matched pair and controls

One pair uses new seed `24140`, prescribed first and minimal second. Both cells
use two principals, `0.033192` starting LLM budget per principal, Luna Medium,
medium reasoning, CLI transport, the structured action schema, zero retries,
no model fallback, and no MCP. Only the cognition package changes.

Provider-free controls must prove:

- exactly fourteen provider dispatches and a trace-free fifteenth rejection;
- exact 7/7 principal distribution;
- an unsuccessful action causes no substitute kernel action;
- mint is advertised and enabled while auxiliary scoring remains impossible;
- terminal receipt/status/checkpoint equality; and
- mutations of budget, seed, condition, call ceiling, distribution, fallback
  policy, or mint availability fail closed.

## Readout and exposure

Primary readout is cell validity and interpretable bilateral trajectory.
Actions, failures, artifacts, paid reads, transfers, balances, and downstream
value are descriptive. One pair has no effect estimate or sampling uncertainty.

`external_call_budget`: 28 Luna calls maximum, 14 per condition, serial width
one, zero retry/repair/fallback/MCP. Attempt-fifteen scarcity checks are
pre-dispatch and do not consume provider calls. Each cell has a stable run ID,
atomic checkpoint, unique acknowledgement, and detached dashboard worker.
Partial evidence is preserved; an invalid first cell suppresses the second.
